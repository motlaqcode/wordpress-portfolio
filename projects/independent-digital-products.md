# Earlier Independent Digital Products

This page documents three independent digital products that I brought to public launch under my own projects. All three were available on public domains while they were operating.

The products used script-based foundations, as noted below. The descriptions focus on the product scope and confirmed work rather than claiming authorship of the underlying scripts.

## Spotifive — Music Streaming Platform

A web-based music streaming platform inspired by Spotify.

- **Product type:** Online music streaming platform
- **Platform foundation:** Script-based
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched

Spotifive was an independent digital product built around online music playback and a streaming-style user experience.

## Serialisho — Movie & TV Series Platform

A dedicated movie and TV series platform accompanied by an Android application.

- **Web platform:** PHP-based script
- **Mobile application:** Android app programmed for the project
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched
- **Commercial outcome:** The project did not continue into sustained commercial operation because sponsorship was not secured.

Serialisho combined a web-based entertainment platform with a companion Android application. The Android application was programmed as part of the project.

## Jilink — Link Management Platform

A web-based link utility offering URL shortening and a multiple-links-in-one-page service.

- **Platform foundation:** PHP script
- **Product scope:** Shortened URLs and consolidated multi-link pages
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched

Jilink provided users with tools to shorten links and collect multiple destinations under a single shareable link.

## Scope and Verification

These are historical products. Their original domains and source code are not currently included as live demos in this portfolio.

The descriptions are based on project history and confirmed details. Since the original code is not available for review at this stage, this page does not claim authorship of the underlying third-party scripts or list unverified implementation details.

Together, these projects show experience taking different web-product concepts to public launch, including a music streaming service, a PHP-based entertainment platform with an Android application, and a link-management service.
