# Earlier Independent Digital Products

This page documents three earlier digital products that I developed, configured, and brought online under my own projects. These products were publicly available on their respective domains at the time.

The original source files are not currently available for technical review, so this overview is intentionally limited to the project details and technology information I can confirm.

## Spotifive — Music Streaming Platform

A web-based music streaming platform inspired by Spotify.

- **Product type:** Online music streaming platform
- **Implementation basis:** Script-based platform
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched

The project involved bringing a dedicated music-streaming service online as an independent digital product.

## Serialisho — Movie & TV Series Platform

A dedicated movie and TV series platform with an Android application.

- **Backend/platform:** PHP-based script
- **Mobile application:** Android app, programmed as part of the project
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched

The project combined a web-based entertainment platform with a companion Android application. The project did not proceed to a sustained commercial operation because external sponsorship was not secured.

## Jilink — Link Management Platform

A web-based link utility offering URL shortening and a multiple-links-in-one-page service.

- **Implementation basis:** PHP script
- **Product features:** Shortened links and consolidated multi-link pages
- **Status:** Completed and publicly launched

Jilink was developed as an independent online service for creating and managing shareable links.

## Scope and Verification

These are historical projects. Their original domains and source code are not currently included as live demos in this portfolio.

The descriptions above are based on project history and confirmed details. Since the original code is not available for review at this stage, this page does not claim authorship of the underlying third-party scripts or list unverified implementation details.

The projects demonstrate experience taking different types of web products from setup and implementation through public launch, including a PHP-based platform and an Android companion application.
