# Motlaq Digital Marketing Agency

**Role:** Founder & WordPress Developer  
**Period:** December 2019 – November 2022  
**Website:** [motlaqagency.ir](https://motlaqagency.ir/) — historical agency website; restoration is in progress.

## Overview

Motlaq was a digital marketing agency I founded and operated, providing website design and development alongside digital marketing services for businesses in different industries.

My work covered both the technical delivery of websites and the broader online presence of client businesses. Depending on the project, this included website planning and implementation, WordPress setup and customization, e-commerce functionality, technical maintenance, SEO, social media page management, content publishing, and advertising support.

The agency website is being restored from a backup. It is included here as the primary reference for the agency's historical portfolio; its current availability and archived content may change during restoration.

## My Role

As the agency founder, I was involved in client communication, project coordination, and hands-on website work. My technical responsibilities included:

- Designing and developing client websites, primarily with WordPress.
- Customizing themes and implementing website features to fit each business.
- Setting up and maintaining e-commerce websites and online sales workflows.
- Handling website-related technical tasks, updates, and troubleshooting.
- Supporting SEO and the ongoing improvement of clients' online presence.
- Coordinating digital marketing work such as social media management, content publishing, and advertising.

## Internal Products and Brands

Alongside client services, I worked on several independent digital products and brand concepts:

- **Jilink:** a PHP-based link shortener and multi-link page service.
- **Spotifive:** a music streaming platform.
- **Serialisho:** a film and series platform; I also programmed its Android application. It was publicly launched, but did not continue as a sustained commercial operation because sponsorship was not secured.
- **Kolahonari:** an independent custom-designed hat sales project.
- **Followber:** an online service for selling Instagram likes and followers.
- **Hostadd:** a hosting-services brand and website.
- **Admineton:** a service brand for managing clients' Instagram pages as an external admin.

A broader Motlaq umbrella structure was also planned, with concepts such as Motlaq Academy, Motlaq Music, Motlaq Design, Motlaq Event, Motlaq News, Motlaq Photography, and Motlaq Podcast. These were planned brand directions and should not be interpreted as fully launched operating divisions.
